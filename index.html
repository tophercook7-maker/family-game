The idea

Think of it as two things in one app.

1. Our family's own place

  • Share photos, videos, and news, just with family.
  • Talk to everybody at once, or just one person.
  • Anything you post can go to Facebook too, with one tap, if you want it to.
  • We can also invite people who are like family, if you want that. You get to decide below.

2. A big family board game, all about us

It plays a little like Monopoly, but the whole game runs on family trivia. Every question is about us: who was born where, whose favorite food is what, what happened that summer at the lake.

No ordinary dice. Instead of rolling two dice, a bubble pops open with a family question. Answer it and you move. Know your family, go further. You'll pick exactly how it works in question 2.
  • The questions come from us. When you sign up, you answer a few quick, easy things about yourself, like your birthday and your favorite food. Then, when we talk and share pictures in the family app, it can turn those moments into new questions. Nothing private goes in unless the person says it's okay.
  • A family bank. Everybody has an account with play money called Pop Bucks. You earn it when you pass Go, answer right, and win games. You pay rent and silly family fines, just like Monopoly.
  • Rent on family places. The squares are places we know, using our own pictures, like your kitchen or the lake. Land on one and buy it. When someone else lands there, they pay you rent.
  • Family fines. "Forgot to call your mom: pay 50." "Late for Sunday dinner: go to Time Out."
  • A Free Pass card, like Monopoly's Get Out of Jail Free. You win it, keep it, use it, or give it to someone you love.
  • Your own game piece. Everyone picks a piece that's theirs, on the board and on their picture.
  • Rewards that mean something, everywhere. Pop Bucks buy new game pieces, prettier boards, and even the right to put your picture on a square.
  • All my games are inside it: Word Popper, PIC POP, and my story games. Playing any of them earns Pop Bucks for the board game. PIC POP is getting a big boost of rewards too, and it can even use our own family pictures as puzzles.

Ways to play

  • Play against family on your own phones, at the same time or one turn at a time over a few days.
  • Play against the computer when nobody's around.
  • Pass one phone around the table. It asks how many people are playing and takes turns for everyone.
